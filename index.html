Climate impact result viewer

Bias and projected change of climate models, calculated with ESMValTool

This application shows results from climate models, calculated with ESMValTool. It is intended to provide some guidance for climate impact researchers, to select one or more datasets that adequately sample the spread of the ensemble.

    Not sure which models to use? Suggest models

    Selects a few models that sample the spread of the projected change, one run of each, after leaving out the most biased models. Check the suggestion in the charts and adjust it to your study.

    Method

    Click a point to select a run, and again to deselect it. Double-click it to select all runs of its model.
