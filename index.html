Climate impact result viewer

Bias and projected change of climate models, calculated with ESMValTool

This application shows results from climate models, calculated with ESMValTool. It is intended to provide some guidance for climate impact researchers, to select one or more datasets that adequately sample the spread of the ensemble.

    Click a point to select a dataset, hold Shift to select multiple, and double-click to clear the selection. Hold Alt to select a range (points will be highlighted in both graphs) and hold Ctrl to pan and zoom.
